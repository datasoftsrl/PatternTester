# Rec.709

## Purpose

Rec.709 defines the standard color space for High Definition television (HDTV).

It specifies:

- primary colors
- white point
- transfer characteristics
- matrix coefficients

Rec.709 remains the reference for most SDR video production.

---

## Typical Uses

- HDTV production
- SDR mastering
- Broadcast
- Blu-ray authoring

---

## Evaluation

Verify:

- primary colors
- grayscale neutrality
- gamma response
- color accuracy

---

## Expected Result

The monitor should reproduce the Rec.709 gamut without clipping or visible hue shifts.

---

## Common Problems

### Incorrect Gamma

Midtones appear too dark or too bright.

---

### White Point Shift

Neutral gray becomes tinted.

---

### Color Errors

Primary colors deviate from reference values.

---

## Professional Notes

Most professional SDR workflows target:

- Rec.709
- Gamma 2.4
- D65 White Point
