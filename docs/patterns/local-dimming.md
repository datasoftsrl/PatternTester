# Local Dimming

## Purpose

Local Dimming patterns evaluate the performance of displays equipped with independently controlled backlight zones.

This includes:

- MiniLED
- Full Array Local Dimming (FALD)
- Advanced LCD televisions

---

## Typical Uses

- Contrast evaluation
- HDR verification
- Blooming analysis

---

## Evaluation

Observe:

- dark backgrounds
- isolated bright objects
- moving bright objects

---

## Expected Result

Bright objects should remain bright without illuminating surrounding dark regions.

---

## Common Problems

### Blooming

Bright objects produce visible halos.

---

### Zone Lag

Backlight reacts too slowly.

---

### Pumping

Brightness changes unexpectedly during motion.

---

## Professional Notes

Perform the evaluation in a completely dark environment.
