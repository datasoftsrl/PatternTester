# White Level

## Purpose

The White Level pattern verifies that the display reproduces highlight information without clipping.

---

# Typical Uses

- Contrast adjustment
- HDR evaluation
- Video calibration

---

# Evaluation

The brightest white should remain distinguishable from the adjacent lighter gray levels.

No highlight detail should disappear.

---

# Common Problems

## White Clipping

Several bright levels merge into pure white.

---

## Blooming

Bright areas illuminate surrounding pixels.

---

## Tone Compression

Highlights lose detail.

---

# Professional Notes

White Level should always be adjusted after Black Level.
