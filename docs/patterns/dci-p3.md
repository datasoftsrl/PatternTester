# DCI-P3

## Purpose

DCI-P3 is a wide color gamut developed for digital cinema.

Today it is commonly used by:

- OLED monitors
- HDR televisions
- Professional video editing
- Modern smartphones

---

## Typical Uses

- HDR mastering
- Cinema production
- Video editing
- Color grading

---

## Evaluation

Inspect:

- saturated reds
- greens
- yellows
- skin tones

---

## Expected Result

Colors should remain saturated while preserving smooth gradients.

---

## Common Problems

### Gamut Compression

Wide colors become clipped.

---

### Incorrect Mapping

The monitor converts colors incorrectly.

---

### Excessive Saturation

Consumer modes exaggerate color intensity.
