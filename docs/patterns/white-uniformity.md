# White Uniformity

## Purpose

White Uniformity evaluates the consistency of brightness and color across a white screen.

---

## Typical Uses

- Professional monitor evaluation
- Print proofing
- Photography
- Color grading

---

## Evaluation

Display a full white image.

Inspect:

- brightness
- tint
- luminance distribution

---

## Expected Result

The display should appear:

- evenly illuminated
- neutral
- free of visible patches

---

## Common Problems

### Corner Darkening

Corners appear darker than the center.

---

### Color Tint

Some regions become:

- warmer
- cooler
- green
- magenta

---

### Bright Spots

Localized brighter areas become visible.

---

## Professional Notes

Professional graphics monitors usually specify uniformity compensation.
