# Backlight Bleeding

## Purpose

Backlight Bleeding patterns reveal unwanted light leakage from the LCD backlight.

This phenomenon does not affect OLED displays.

---

## Typical Uses

- LCD inspection
- Manufacturing quality control
- Customer acceptance testing

---

## Evaluation

Display a completely black fullscreen pattern.

Inspect:

- corners
- edges
- center

---

## Expected Result

The screen should appear uniformly black.

---

## Common Problems

### Corner Bleeding

Light leakage near corners.

---

### Edge Bleeding

Bright regions appear along display borders.

---

### Pressure Spots

Localized bright areas caused by panel stress.

---

## Professional Notes

Backlight bleeding should always be evaluated:

- in a dark room
- after monitor warm-up
- at normal brightness settings
