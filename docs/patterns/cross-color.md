# Cross Color

## Purpose

Cross Color patterns reveal unwanted interactions between luminance and chrominance.

They are primarily useful for composite video systems and legacy analog equipment.

---

# Typical Uses

- Composite video testing
- Broadcast equipment
- Legacy video processors
- Retro gaming hardware

---

# Evaluation

The image should remain:

- stable
- free of false colors
- free of shimmering
- free of moving artifacts

---

# Common Problems

## False Color

Fine luminance details produce unwanted colors.

---

## Dot Crawl

Moving color artifacts appear along high-contrast edges.

---

## Chroma Interference

Color information leaks into luminance detail.

---

# Professional Notes

Cross Color evaluation is mainly applicable to composite video sources.

Digital HDMI connections normally do not exhibit these artifacts.
