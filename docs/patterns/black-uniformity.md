# Black Uniformity

## Purpose

Black Uniformity evaluates how consistently the display reproduces black across the entire panel.

---

## Typical Uses

- LCD quality control
- OLED inspection
- HDR verification

---

## Evaluation

Display a full black screen.

Inspect:

- center
- corners
- edges

---

## Expected Result

The screen should appear uniformly dark.

---

## Common Problems

### Clouding

Random brighter regions become visible.

---

### Backlight Leakage

Light escapes from panel edges.

---

### Panel Glow

Brightness changes depending on viewing angle.

---

## Professional Notes

Allow the monitor to warm up before testing.

Ambient lighting should be minimized.
