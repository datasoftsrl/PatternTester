# Grayscale

## Purpose

The grayscale pattern evaluates the monitor's ability to reproduce different luminance levels without clipping, crushing or banding.

---

## Typical Uses

- Brightness adjustment
- Contrast adjustment
- Gamma verification
- Black level evaluation
- White level evaluation

---

## What to Look For

A correctly calibrated display should show:

- distinguishable dark shades
- distinguishable bright shades
- smooth transitions
- no visible banding

---

## Common Problems

### Black Crush

Dark gray levels become completely black.

Possible causes:

- excessive contrast
- incorrect gamma
- monitor calibration

---

### White Clipping

Bright levels merge into pure white.

Possible causes:

- excessive brightness
- HDR configuration
- graphics driver settings

---

### Banding

Visible steps instead of smooth gradients.

Possible causes:

- low color depth
- incorrect gamma
- panel limitations

---

## Professional Notes

Always evaluate grayscale after the monitor has reached operating temperature.

Ambient light should remain constant throughout the evaluation.
