# Color Temperature

## Purpose

Color Temperature patterns evaluate the display white point.

The white point defines the chromatic appearance of neutral colors.

---

# Typical Uses

- Display calibration
- Factory quality control
- Color critical applications
- Photography
- Video production

---

# Typical White Points

| Temperature | Typical Use |
|-------------|-------------|
| 5000 K | Printing |
| 6500 K | Standard monitor |
| 9300 K | Consumer televisions |

---

# Evaluation

Neutral gray should remain free of:

- blue tint
- red tint
- green tint

---

# Common Problems

## Blue White

Typical of factory monitor presets.

---

## Warm White

Visible yellow or red cast.

---

## Green Tint

Usually indicates poor calibration.

---

# Professional Notes

The industry standard white point for most computer monitors is D65 (6500 K).
