# Siemens Star

## Purpose

The Siemens Star pattern evaluates image sharpness and optical resolution.

It consists of radial black and white sectors converging toward a common center.

This pattern is widely used in:

- monitor evaluation
- projector testing
- camera lens testing
- optical systems

---

# Typical Uses

- Focus adjustment
- Resolution analysis
- Lens evaluation
- Scaling verification

---

# Evaluation

Observe the center of the star.

A correctly reproduced image should show:

- clean radial lines
- symmetric convergence
- minimal aliasing
- no false colors

---

# Common Problems

## Loss of Resolution

The center becomes a solid gray circle.

---

## Aliasing

Jagged radial edges indicate insufficient sampling.

---

## Chromatic Aberration

Colored fringes appear around high-contrast transitions.

---

# Professional Notes

Evaluate the pattern both at normal viewing distance and close inspection.

Scaling and image enhancement may significantly affect the result.
