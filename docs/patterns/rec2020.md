# Rec.2020

## Purpose

Rec.2020 defines the Ultra High Definition television color space.

It supports significantly wider color reproduction than Rec.709.

---

## Typical Uses

- UHD production
- HDR mastering
- 4K
- 8K

---

## Evaluation

Inspect:

- highly saturated colors
- gradient smoothness
- clipping
- tone mapping

---

## Expected Result

Wide gamut colors should remain distinguishable throughout the visible range.

---

## Common Problems

### Gamut Compression

Wide colors become clipped.

---

### Tone Mapping Errors

HDR conversion produces inaccurate colors.

---

### Color Volume Reduction

Bright saturated colors lose intensity.

---

## Professional Notes

Few displays currently reproduce the complete Rec.2020 color space.
