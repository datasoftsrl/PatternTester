# Chroma Resolution

## Purpose

Chroma Resolution evaluates the display's ability to reproduce fine color detail independently from luminance detail.

---

## Typical Uses

- Video processor evaluation
- Broadcast equipment
- HDMI verification
- Compression testing

---

## Evaluation

Inspect colored fine lines and alternating color structures.

Color transitions should remain sharp.

---

## Expected Result

The monitor should reproduce thin colored details without smearing.

---

## Common Problems

### Chroma Blur

Fine color detail becomes soft.

---

### Color Bleeding

Adjacent colors merge together.

---

### Incorrect Chroma Upsampling

Compression artifacts become visible.
