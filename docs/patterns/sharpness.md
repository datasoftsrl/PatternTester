# Sharpness

## Purpose

Sharpness patterns evaluate the ability of the display to reproduce fine details.

---

## Typical Uses

- focus adjustment
- monitor sharpness setting
- scaling verification
- image processing evaluation

---

## Expected Result

The image should appear:

- crisp
- stable
- free of halos
- without artificial enhancement

---

## Common Problems

### Excessive Sharpness

Typical symptoms:

- white halos
- black halos
- ringing

---

### Low Sharpness

Typical symptoms:

- blurred edges
- reduced detail
- loss of texture

---

## Professional Notes

Most monitors should be evaluated with image enhancement disabled.
